Kumbhmela News

The Kumbhmela is the largest religious gathering in the world, attracting millions of devotees from across the globe. This event is held every 12 years at four different locations in India: Prayagraj, Haridwar, Nashik, and Ujjain. The Kumbhmela is a time for spiritual cleansing, where pilgrims take a dip in the holy rivers to wash away their sins. The festival also features various religious ceremonies, cultural performances, and discourses by spiritual leaders.
